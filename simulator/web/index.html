Pairs

On their own not in the matches

Each pair is a match between its two currencies: EUR/USD up means EUR beat USD today; USD/JPY down means JPY beat USD. A currency's score is how many of its three matches it won, and equal scores go to the bigger average move (each pair's move taken from that currency's side). The day opens at 17:00 New York, like the daily candle of a broker on New York + 7 h; a move is the Bid now against the day's first Bid. A replay is measured at its own clock, never later.
