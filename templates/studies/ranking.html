By concept

Ranked on the ratio — how many times its own matched-normal move the pair made in this window — not on the pip move. A pip means something different in USDJPY than in EURUSD, so pooling raw magnitudes across pairs would rank the volatile pairs rather than the news. 1.0× is an ordinary window: the ratio is the mean move over the mean normal move, and reads 1.0 at hours when nothing was released.

The two columns answer different questions and the order follows the median. The median is what a typical release in the family is worth; the strongest is what the family's heaviest release can do. They can be far apart, and when they are it is the concept's composition talking: labour holds weekly Unemployment Claims, ADP and JOLTS alongside payrolls, and each of those contributes seven pairings near 0.9× that pull the median down without saying anything about what payrolls do. Read the median for breadth and the strongest for ceiling — neither alone is "how much this kind of news matters".

Published rank is the mean of the calendar's own high/medium/low rating for the indicators in that concept, turned into a rank so a 1–3 scale and a ratio can be compared at all. It is their claim, kept as theirs.

By indicator

Direction is the third mode: the signed response to the indicator coming in above its own previous print, in abnormal return per one sigma of change. It is not a surprise — part of any change was already expected and priced, so the coefficient is attenuated against what a true consensus forecast would give. Blank means no direction curve survived the false-discovery correction at this horizon, which is the common case and the honest one.

What this ranking is not. Every figure is an association under controls — the tool observes what follows a release against the pair's own matched-normal behaviour, and cannot rule out a common driver or unobserved news (§3.6). It also cannot separate releases that share an instant: where a release group exists, the move belongs to the report, never to one member of it (§3.3 Case B). Speeches and political events are absent because they carry no number and no timestamp in this dataset, not because they were measured and found inert.
