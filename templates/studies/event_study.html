How to read this

ESTIMATE
Every number here is measured from this sample. It is not a constant of nature and it is not a forecast.
ASSOCIATION
The tool observes what follows a release under controls. It cannot rule out common drivers or unobserved news, so the honest claim is association, never causation.
NOT SEPARABLE
Releases sharing a timestamp cannot be told apart — payrolls, the unemployment rate and average hourly earnings land together, so this measures the whole report, not one number in it.
RESOLUTION
Measured on {{ engine_version }} against hourly bars, so rungs shorter than an hour are absent rather than interpolated.
